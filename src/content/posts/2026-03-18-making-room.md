Most useful systems begin with subtraction. This note is about the relief that appears when a busy page, a crowded desk, or a noisy routine loses one unnecessary thing.

The work is not always dramatic. It can look like clearing a single corner, deciding not to answer immediately, or choosing a structure that leaves room for thought to arrive.

When a project feels stuck, I often return to that small question: what can be removed without hurting the thing itself?
