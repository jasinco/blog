A notebook works best when it remains slightly unfinished. It should hold fragments, quotations, half-built arguments, and notes that have not yet found their neighbours.

That is what makes it useful: a commonplace book is not a vault. It is a table where unrelated things can be left in the same room until they begin to speak to one another.

The value is not in completeness. It is in return.
