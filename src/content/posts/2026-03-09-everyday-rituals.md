A life becomes legible through repetition. Not the grand routines that look good in a calendar, but the small gestures that quietly return each day.

The cup rinsed before coffee. The long walk after rain. The notebook opened before the screen. These are not habits with an audience; they are ways of making the day inhabitable.

I keep a list of them because ordinary rituals disappear quickly unless they are written down.
